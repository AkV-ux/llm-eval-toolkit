Sunrise Electronics - Customer Policy

Returns: Products can be returned within 14 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5 business days after we receive the item.

Warranty: All laptops carry a 2-year warranty. Phones and tablets carry a 1-year warranty. Accessories carry a 6-month warranty. Water damage is not covered.

Shipping: Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 299 rupees extra. Orders above 5000 rupees get free standard shipping.

Support: Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM. Email: help@sunriseelectronics.example. Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. After shipping, the return policy applies.
